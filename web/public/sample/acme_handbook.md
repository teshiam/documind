# Acme Robotics Employee Handbook

## Leave policy

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Unused leave
can be carried over, up to a maximum of 5 days, into the first quarter of the next year.

Sick leave is separate from annual leave. Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per
year, and a doctor's note is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Core collaboration
hours are 10:00 to 15:00 Central European Time, and all remote staff must be reachable then.

## Equipment and expenses

New hires receive a laptop and a 400 euro home-office stipend in their first month.
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of purchase through the Finance Portal.
Flights must be booked at least 14 days in advance unless a manager grants an exception.

## Security

Passwords must be at least 14 characters and rotated every 180 days. Company laptops must use
full-disk encryption, and security incidents must be reported to security@acme.example within
24 hours of discovery.
